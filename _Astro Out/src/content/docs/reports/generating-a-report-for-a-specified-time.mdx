To generate a report for a specified time, do the following:

1. Log in to the BearQ application.
2. Click **Reports**.

   ![Reports tab](../../../assets/reports/generating-a-report-for-a-specified-time/Reports_tab.png)
3. Click **Generate Daily Report**.

   ![Generate daily report](../../../assets/reports/generating-a-report-for-a-specified-time/Generate_daily_report.png)
4. Select the time range for the report.

   - You can either select the time range in the recent time window. Click **Last X Hours** and choose the time from 1 to 24 hours.

     ![X hours report](../../../assets/reports/generating-a-report-for-a-specified-time/X_hours_report.png)
   - Click **Specific Date & Time** and choose the specific date and time for which you want to generate your report.

     ![Specific time report](../../../assets/reports/generating-a-report-for-a-specified-time/Specific_time_report.png)
5. Click **Generate Report**.

   ![Generate report on time](../../../assets/reports/generating-a-report-for-a-specified-time/Generate_report_on_time.png)

Your report will be generated.
